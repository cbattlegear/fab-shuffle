                        What this report does and does not check

                            Connections pointing at the source workspace

                            A tenant-wide scan of every connection this credential can see, for one whose path still names the source workspace, one of its items, or one of its endpoints. Reporting only: Fabric does not let a connection's target be changed through the API, so repoint or recreate one listed here by hand, then update whatever uses it. The original connection is kept listed even where a replacement was mapped, since something outside this migration may still read through it.

                            What this scan does and does not check

                                Look up connection names

                                Runs signed in as you, not the service principal, and only reads: it grants, creates or changes nothing, and looking a name up here does not give you or the service principal any access to that connection. Works even without a recorded scan - it then lists every connection you can see instead of looking up specific ids.
